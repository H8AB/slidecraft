# Ecommerce example verification

The creation path reads deck.json and the product images to generate 01-created.pptx. The editing path applies plan.json to 02-before.pptx and produces 03-optimized.pptx.

The three English decks were rendered with LibreOffice and all 15 slides were reviewed visually. The redesigned deck retains the original draft's slide text and notes. The chart and embedded workbook on slide 3 and the three-row native table on slide 4 remain editable.

The displayed click-through rates are 720 / 20,000 = 3.6% and 900 / 20,000 = 4.5%. The absolute difference is 0.9 percentage points. The relative increase is 25%. Orders per click are 36 / 720 and 45 / 900, both 5.0%.
